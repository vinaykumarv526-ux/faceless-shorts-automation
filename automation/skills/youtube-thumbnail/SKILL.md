# YouTube Thumbnail

## Step 1. Apply thumbnail best practices

Every thumbnail must follow these rules:
- **One clear focal element**: Clean 2D technical vector cutaway, glowing schematic prop, or high-contrast subject filling 40-50% of the frame. Readable at 320px mobile size.
- **3 to 5 words maximum** of massive, bold sans-serif text.
- **Two colours dominate**: Dark blueprint navy (`#0A121F`) + high-contrast accent (`#FFD43B` yellow, `#D62839` danger red, or `#4FD1E8` cyan).
- **High contrast** between subject, text, and background.
- **Text is a punchy hook phrase**, NOT a repetition of the title.
- **No text in bottom-right corner** (where YouTube watch time badge sits).
- Dimensions: 1280 x 720 pixels (16:9) or 1080 x 1920 (9:16 Shorts cover frame).
